Canvas Privacy Policy Last updated: November 11, 2026 Developer: Shivank Tech This Privacy Policy explains how Canvas, developed by Shivank Tech, handles information when you use the Canvas mobile application. Information We Collect Canvas is primarily a photo and video editing application. Depending on the features you use, the app may request access to photos, videos, media files, or other device features required to provide its editing functionality. Canvas does not require you to create an account to use its core editing features. Photos and Videos Canvas may request access to photos and videos on your device so that you can select media for editing. Media you select is used to provide the editing features you request, such as editing, applying effects, adjustments, retouching, cropping, and exporting. We do not claim ownership of your photos or videos. Device Permissions Canvas may request permissions that are necessary for features provided by the application. Permissions may include access to photos and videos, storage or media files, and other permissions required by specific features. You can manage application permissions through your device settings. Advertising Canvas may display advertisements through third-party advertising services. Third-party advertising providers may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and usage information in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings. In-App Purchases Canvas may offer optional subscriptions or other paid features through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play. Canvas does not receive or store your payment card information. Third-Party Services Canvas may use third-party services to provide application functionality, analytics, advertising, payments, hosting, or other services. These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. How We Use Information Information and device permissions are used only as necessary to: * Provide and operate Canvas features * Allow you to select and edit photos and videos * Process and export your media * Provide optional paid features * Display advertisements where applicable * Maintain and improve the application * Detect and prevent abuse or security issues Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Canvas. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Data Retention Canvas does not intentionally retain your personal photos or videos on our servers unless a specific feature requires such processing. Media stored locally on your device remains under your control. Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to those providers’ respective policies. Children’s Privacy Canvas is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

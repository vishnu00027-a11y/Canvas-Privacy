Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Canvas, developed by Shivank Tech, handles information when you use the Canvas mobile application.

By using Canvas, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Canvas is primarily a photo and video editing application. Depending on the features you use, the application may request access to photos, videos, media files, or other device features required to provide its functionality.

Canvas does not require you to create an account to use its core editing features.

We do not intentionally collect personal information simply because you install or use Canvas.

2. Photos and Videos

Canvas may request access to photos and videos on your device so that you can select media for editing.

Photos and videos you select are processed to provide the editing features you request, such as adjustments, effects, retouching, presets, cropping, transformations, and exporting.

Your photos and videos remain your property. Shivank Tech does not claim ownership of content that you create, import, edit, or export using Canvas.

3. Device Permissions

Canvas may request permissions that are necessary for specific features of the application.

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, these may include access to photos and videos, media files, camera functionality, or other device capabilities.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings.

4. Advertising

Canvas may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and advertising or usage information, depending on their services and your device settings.

Third-party advertising providers operate under their own privacy policies. You can manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Canvas may offer optional paid features, subscriptions, or other in-app purchases through Google Play.

Payment transactions are processed by Google Play. Canvas does not receive or store your payment card details.

Information related to purchases may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

6. Third-Party Services

Canvas may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, including advertising, payment processing, analytics, hosting, security, or other application services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Shivank Tech does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

7. How We Use Information

Information and permissions associated with Canvas may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Canvas features.
  • Allow you to select and edit photos and videos.
  • Process and export your media.
  • Provide optional paid features and subscriptions.
  • Display advertisements where applicable.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Improve application functionality and performance.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Canvas does not intentionally store your personal photos or videos on Shivank Tech servers as part of the application's normal editing workflow.

Media files created or selected through Canvas may remain on your device according to your device's storage settings and your actions.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the respective policies of those services.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Canvas.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Canvas is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

11. Your Choices

You can control application permissions through your Android device settings.

You can also stop using Canvas and uninstall the application at any time.

Advertising and certain privacy preferences may also be controlled through your Android or Google account settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
